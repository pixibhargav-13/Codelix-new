Short answer: E-commerce website development cost in India starts around $3,000–$8,000 for a Shopify or WooCommerce store set up with your products, payments and shipping, $8,000–$20,000 for a custom store with ERP or inventory sync and GST invoicing, and above $20,000 for multi-vendor marketplaces or high-volume stores. These are our published ranges.

Selling online looks simple from the outside, but a store that actually converts has a lot underneath it: product pages that load fast on a phone, UPI and card payments that confirm reliably, stock that stays in sync and invoices that satisfy GST rules. This guide explains what each level of store includes and what it costs to run afterwards.

## Shopify vs WooCommerce vs a custom store

| Option | Upfront cost | Running cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopify | Lower | Monthly plan plus app subscriptions | Brands that want to launch fast and grow without managing servers |
| WooCommerce | Lower | Hosting plus paid plugins | Businesses already on WordPress or wanting full control of content |
| Custom store | Higher | Hosting and maintenance | Complex pricing, B2B ordering, deep ERP integration or marketplaces |

For most new D2C brands, Shopify is the fastest route to a reliable store. A custom build makes sense when your business rules don't fit a platform — dealer pricing, bulk ordering, multiple warehouses or a marketplace with seller payouts.

## What drives the cost

1. Catalogue size and variants. A hundred products with sizes and colours need more setup than twenty simple ones.
2. Payment gateways. Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, UPI and cash on delivery each need integration and testing, including refunds.
3. Shipping and logistics. Shiprocket or courier integrations, pin-code serviceability and tracking pages.
4. GST invoicing. Correct tax calculation, HSN codes and invoice generation for every order.
5. Inventory sync. Connecting the store to your ERP, Tally or warehouse system so stock never oversells.
6. Custom features. Subscriptions, wholesale pricing, product builders or loyalty programmes.

## Running costs after launch

A store has ongoing costs that a brochure website doesn't:

- Platform fees, such as your Shopify plan, or hosting for WooCommerce and custom stores
- Payment gateway charges on each transaction
- Paid apps and plugins for reviews, upsells, search and marketing
- SMS, email and WhatsApp messages for order updates
- Maintenance, updates and small changes — our maintenance plans start at $500–$1,500 a month

## How long it takes to launch

A platform store typically launches in 3–6 weeks once products and content are ready. A custom store with integrations usually takes 8–14 weeks. Gathering product photos, descriptions and prices is often the slowest part, so start on it early.

## How to spend your e-commerce budget wisely

- Start on Shopify or WooCommerce unless you have a clear reason to go custom.
- Spend on fast mobile pages and a simple checkout before fancy features.
- Integrate inventory early if you also sell offline, to avoid overselling.
- Keep the number of paid apps low; each one adds cost and slows the store.
- Make sure you own the store, domain and customer data.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum cost of an e-commerce website in India?

A professionally set up Shopify or WooCommerce store starts around $3,000–$8,000 on our published pricing, plus platform and gateway fees.

### Is Shopify or WooCommerce cheaper?

WooCommerce has no platform fee but needs hosting and paid plugins; Shopify charges a monthly plan but handles hosting and security. Over a year they often cost a similar amount.

### When should I build a custom e-commerce website?

When your rules don't fit a platform: dealer pricing, bulk B2B orders, multi-warehouse stock, or a marketplace with many sellers.

### Can my online store connect to Tally or my ERP?

Yes. Orders, stock and invoices can sync between the store and Tally or an ERP, so your accounts and inventory stay accurate.

### How long does it take to build an online store?

A platform store usually takes 3–6 weeks; a custom store with integrations takes 8–14 weeks.
