Short answer: Custom software development cost in India ranges from about $10,000–$25,000 for a focused business system, to $25,000–$60,000 for a multi-module platform, and above $60,000 for company-wide or enterprise systems. These are our published bands; smaller tools and automations can cost less, and the exact price depends on scope, integrations and timeline.

Custom software is built around how your business works rather than forcing you into someone else's product. That flexibility is the point — and it is also why pricing varies so much. This guide explains how software projects are priced in India, what you get at each level and how to compare quotes fairly.

## Fixed price, hourly or dedicated team?

| Pricing model | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed price | Scope, price and timeline agreed in writing before work starts | Projects with clear requirements |
| Milestone-based | A fixed project paid in stages as each part is delivered | Larger builds delivered in phases |
| Dedicated team | Monthly cost for developers who work only on your product | Ongoing products with a changing backlog |

Fixed pricing protects your budget, but only if the scope is written down properly. Dedicated teams give flexibility when priorities change week to week. Many companies start with a fixed-price first version and move to a dedicated team once the product is live.

## What decides where your project lands

1. Scope. The number of modules, screens, reports and workflows is the largest driver.
2. Integrations. Connections to Tally, payment gateways, GST portals, CRMs or legacy systems each add work.
3. User roles and permissions. More roles mean more screens, rules and testing.
4. Data migration. Moving old data from spreadsheets or legacy software needs cleaning and verification.
5. Non-functional needs. Audit trails, high availability, security reviews and performance under heavy load.
6. Timeline. A tight deadline means more developers working in parallel, which raises the cost.

## Why India-based development costs less

Development teams in India cost significantly less than equivalent teams in the US, UK or Australia, which is why so many companies outsource here. The saving is real, but it only holds if the work is right the first time. Look for clear written scope, regular demos, direct access to the people building your software, and full ownership of the code.

## How to get an accurate quote

- Describe the problem and the people who will use the software, not just a feature list.
- Share examples of the spreadsheets, forms or tools you use today.
- List every system the software must connect to.
- Say what must be in the first release and what can wait.
- Ask each vendor what is excluded, who owns the code and what support costs after launch.

## Costs after launch

Budget for hosting, third-party services and maintenance. Most systems need regular updates, fixes and small improvements once people start using them every day; our maintenance plans start at $500–$1,500 a month. Feature work after launch is usually priced per change or covered by a dedicated team.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does custom software cost in India?

On our published pricing, focused business systems start around $10,000–$25,000, with larger platforms from $25,000–$60,000 and above.

### Is fixed price or hourly better?

Fixed price is better when requirements are clear, because the budget is protected. A dedicated team suits products whose priorities change often.

### Why do quotes from different companies vary so much?

They often assume different scopes, quality levels and support. Compare what each quote includes and excludes, not just the total.

### How long does custom software take to build?

A focused system usually takes 6–12 weeks. Larger platforms are typically delivered in phases over several months.

### Who owns the software after it's built?

You should own the code, data and accounts outright. Make sure the contract says so before you sign.
