Short answer: Website development cost in India for a professionally built business website starts around $1,500–$5,000 for a 5–15 page site with a CMS, $5,000–$10,000 for a custom-designed site or web app with logins and integrations, and $10,000–$25,000 for large portals with many roles and workflows. These are our published ranges; templates and freelancers can be cheaper, with trade-offs.

You can get a website in India for almost any budget, from a few thousand rupees on a template to lakhs for a custom platform. The difference is what the site does for your business: whether it loads fast, ranks on Google, converts visitors and can grow without a rebuild. This guide explains what you get at each level.

## What drives the price

1. Custom design vs template. A design made for your brand costs more than a purchased theme but loads faster and stands out.
2. Number of pages and templates. Ten pages on three layouts cost less than ten pages that each need their own design.
3. Content management. A CMS your team can edit easily takes setup time, but saves you calling a developer for every change.
4. Integrations. CRM, booking systems, payment gateways, WhatsApp and marketing tools each need to be connected and tested.
5. Logins and user accounts. Anything behind a login — customer portals, dashboards, member areas — turns a website into a web application.
6. Performance and SEO. Fast loading, clean code, schema markup and good Core Web Vitals take care to build in, and they decide whether the site ranks.

## Template, freelancer or agency?

| Option | Cost | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| DIY template or website builder | Lowest | Slow pages, generic look, limited SEO control |
| Freelancer | Low to medium | Availability for fixes later, no backup if they move on |
| Development company | Medium to high | Make sure scope, price and ownership are in writing |

Whichever you choose, insist on owning your domain, hosting account and admin login. A surprising number of businesses discover they can't change their own website because someone else controls the accounts.

## How long website development takes

A business website typically takes 3–6 weeks from agreed content to launch. A custom web application takes 6–12 weeks or more depending on features. The most common delay isn't development — it's waiting for content, photos and approvals, so prepare those early.

## Ongoing costs to budget for

- Domain renewal every year
- Hosting, from shared hosting for small sites to cloud servers for web apps
- SSL is usually free today, but some hosts still charge for it
- Maintenance: updates, security patches, backups and small changes
- Content and SEO work to keep the site ranking after launch

## How to get more website for your budget

- Write down what the site must do — enquiries, bookings, sales — before asking for quotes.
- Prepare your content and photos in advance.
- Launch the core pages first and add sections later.
- Choose speed over effects; heavy animations slow pages down and hurt rankings.
- Get a written quote that lists pages, features and what happens after launch.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average website development cost in India?

For a professionally built business website, our published range is $1,500–$5,000. Custom web applications start around $5,000.

### Why do website quotes vary so much?

Because they cover different things: custom design or a template, how many pages, integrations, SEO setup and support after launch. Compare quotes on scope, not just price.

### How long does it take to build a website?

A business website usually takes 3–6 weeks once content is ready. Custom web applications take 6–12 weeks or more.

### Do I need to pay for maintenance?

Not necessarily, but someone needs to apply updates, renew the domain and keep backups. A maintenance plan is cheaper than fixing a hacked or broken site.

### Will my website rank on Google?

A well-built site gives you the foundation — speed, structure and technical SEO — but rankings also depend on content and links built over time.
